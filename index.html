Math Clash: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Math Clash ("the game") is a math tug-of-war game for Android published by Md Farhan Labib. This page explains what the game does and does not do with your data.

The short version

Math Clash works offline and has no account system of its own. It uses Google services for optional rewarded ads, anonymous usage analytics, crash reports and the optional "Remove ads" purchase, described below. The developer does not receive your name, email or contact details.

Data stored on your device

Your scores, knots, unlocked skins, settings and match history are stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games from the game's Settings, the game uses Google Play Games Services to save your progress to the cloud, submit scores to leaderboards and unlock achievements. This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The developer receives no personal data from this feature. If you do not sign in, nothing is sent.

Playing a friend on another phone (optional)

The "Play a friend" mode connects two nearby phones directly using Google Nearby Connections (Bluetooth and Wi-Fi). Only the match moves are exchanged between the two phones. Nothing goes through a developer server. The game asks for the Nearby/Bluetooth/location-related permissions only to find and connect to the other phone; your location is never read or stored.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch a short ad to earn in-game Knots. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID and similar identifiers to show and measure ads, according to your consent choice (shown in a Google consent form where required). You can change your choice from Settings > Privacy and consent. The "Remove ads" purchase turns ads off. See the Google advertising policy.

Analytics, crash reports and remote settings (Firebase)

The game uses Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config to learn which features are used, to fix crashes, and to adjust settings such as reward amounts. This includes app events (for example, a match finished), device model, OS version, app version and an app-instance identifier, but no names or contact details. See the Firebase privacy information.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. The developer only learns that the purchase happened; payment details stay with Google.

Children

The game is intended for ages 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
